## 🧠 Learning Methodology

I follow a practical approach to learning cybersecurity.

### 1. 📖 Learn

Study concepts through learning resources such as courses, lectures, documentation, books, and other educational material.

### 2. 🧠 Understand

Break down the concepts and focus on understanding **how and why** they work rather than simply memorizing them.

### 3. 💻 Practice

Apply what I learn through commands, tools, code, configurations, and practical exercises.

### 4. 🧪 Experiment

Test my understanding through controlled and authorized practical environments.

### 5. 📝 Document

Record what I learned, what I practiced, what I observed, and what I discovered.

### 6. 🔍 Review

Identify mistakes, knowledge gaps, and concepts that require further understanding or revision.

### 7. 🚀 Improve

Strengthen my knowledge through additional practice and progressively deeper learning.
